# Andalusian Residences — Interactive 3D Tour & Virtual Staging

Interactive 3D architectural explorer, Modern Mediterranean AI-staged virtual tour, Before/After room studio, "Choose Your Own Kitchen" configurator, and HD 360° Walk-Around & Drone-Style Fly-Through videos for the 3-storey Andalusian apartment development.

## Included in v1 (Apartment 3 — Penthouse Showcase)
- **Interactive 3D Dollhouse & Drone Flight (Three.js):** Orbit all 3 floors, toggle Penthouse Cutaway vs. Full Exterior, click room hotspots, or run the automated drone fly-through spline.
- **10 AI-Staged Modern Mediterranean Penthouse Views + Building Exterior:** Before/After comparison slider & 360° interactive pan viewer covering the Open-Plan Lounge, Living & Dining Panorama, Fitted Designer Kitchen, Wrap-Around Sea-View Terrace (2 angles), Master Suite, En-Suite, Family Spa Bathroom, Kitted-Out Utility Room (washer/dryer + boiler + oak shelves), and Guest Bedroom.
- **Choose Your Own Kitchen Configurator:** Toggle between the **Large L-Shaped (4.0m × 2.6m)** and **Compact Peninsula (4.4m × 3.0m)** layouts in white gloss PET slab & honed matt black granite across 5 photoreal angles each.
- **HD Tour Videos:** Stream fullscreen or download `penthouse-walkaround-360.mp4` and `penthouse-drone-flythrough.mp4`.
- **All 3 Floors Specifications:** Complete specs for Apartments 1, 2, and 3 (Penthouse).
